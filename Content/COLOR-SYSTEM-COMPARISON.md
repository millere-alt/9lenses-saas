## Why We Recommend the 9Vectors Colors

### 1. Authenticity
Your 9Vectors book is already in the market. Using those exact colors creates immediate visual consistency between your book and digital platforms.

### 2. Recognition  
Customers who've seen your book will instantly recognize Light Cyan (#A8D8E8) as "9Vectors" - building brand equity faster.

### 3. Professional Heritage
The cyan/teal palette conveys trust, intelligence, and professionalism - perfect for B2B enterprise software.

### 4. Differentiation
Most SaaS companies use blue/orange. The cyan/teal makes you stand out while remaining professional.

### 5. Proven System
These colors have already been tested in your published book - you know they work together.
